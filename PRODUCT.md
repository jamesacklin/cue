# Cue

A compact, keyboard-accessible macOS player for a Plex music library, in the spirit of foobar2000. Keep artist, album, track and queue views textual. Prioritize a low idle footprint, bounded library browsing, and uninterrupted direct playback.

The client uses AppKit on macOS 14 and later. Plex handles library-wide searches. AVFoundation streams the selected audio. A small playable library makes the first launch usable before connecting an account.
